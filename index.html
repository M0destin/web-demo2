Our Process

Four stages, each signed off with you before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit the site, listen to your brief and talk through budget, priorities and timing.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Drawings, permits, a written quote and a build programme you can hold us to.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Our team builds to plan, keeps the site safe and tidy, and keeps you updated.

  4. 04

    Handover

    A joint walk-through, any snags fixed, then documents and keys handed over.
