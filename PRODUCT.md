# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no build step. Files: `index.html`, `about.html`, `projects.html`, `contact.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/site.js` (shared header/footer/CTA, company details, projects list, default language), `js/main.js` (behaviour), `js/translations.js` (Lithuanian), `assets/images/`. The owner will keep editing the code by hand, so it must stay clean, well commented and easy to change, with design tokens (colours, fonts, spacing) as CSS variables in one place.

## Users

A mix of private homeowners (new houses, extensions, renovations) and commercial clients (businesses, developers, property owners), roughly equal. Both arrive while sizing up builders for a specific job and want to judge whether this company can be trusted with it before asking for a quote.

## Product Purpose

The marketing website for CONSTRE SOLUTIONS, a construction company. Its job is to earn trust and turn visitors into quote requests. Success means a qualified visitor submits the contact or quote form.

## Positioning

- **Finish quality:** known for detail and craftsmanship in the finished work.
- **Experience:** a team with over 20 years of experience.

Not claimed (do not add without confirmation): fixed-price quotes, a single project manager from start to finish, in-house design and build.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare several contractors, look at past work, check who they would be dealing with, then get in touch. The site is single-page with smooth-scroll navigation.

Planned sections from the original brief: Header, Hero, Services, About (with stat counters), Projects (filterable: Residential / Commercial / Renovation), Process (Consultation → Planning → Construction → Handover), Testimonials, Call-to-action band, Contact (form, company details, map), Footer.

Services listed: Residential Construction, Commercial Buildings, Renovation, Project Management, Interior Finishing, Design & Planning.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the work.** Finish quality is the main claim, so real project photography carries the argument. Layouts should let photos be big and close enough to see detail.
2. **Earned trust over claims.** Use only confirmed facts. Placeholders stay visibly marked until real content replaces them.
3. **Serve both audiences.** Homeowners and commercial clients should each see their kind of work quickly.
4. **Every path leads to a quote.** The quote request is always within reach.
5. **Owner-editable.** Plain, commented code; content, images and colours easy to swap.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, alt text on images, good colour contrast, and visible focus states for keyboard users (from the owner's brief).
